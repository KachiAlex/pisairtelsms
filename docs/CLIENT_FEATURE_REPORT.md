# Pisairtel-Schools — School Management System
## Comprehensive Feature Report for Client Proposal

---

### What Is Pisairtel-Schools?

Pisairtel-Schools is a complete, cloud-based school management platform designed to run every aspect of a modern school — from admissions and academics to finance, communications, and digital learning. It replaces fragmented spreadsheets, paper records, and disconnected software with one unified system accessible from any device, anywhere.

The platform serves **five types of users**, each with a tailored experience:

- **School Administrators** — full control over all school operations
- **Teachers & Staff** — tools for teaching, grading, attendance, and communication
- **Students** — a personal portal for assignments, results, exams, and learning
- **Parents** — real-time visibility into their child's academic progress and school life
- **Platform Owners** — oversight across multiple schools (multi-tenant architecture)

## 1. Admissions & Student Enrollment

### Online Application Portal
Prospective students can apply online through a public application form available on your school's website — no login required. The form captures all necessary applicant information and feeds directly into your admissions pipeline.

### Online Inquiry Management
Parents and prospective students can submit inquiries online. All inquiries are captured in the system for follow-up, ensuring no potential enrollment falls through the cracks.

### Student Enrollment Workflow
Once an application is approved, the system guides staff through a structured enrollment process — assigning the student to a class, generating a student ID, and creating their portal accounts automatically.

### Bulk Student Import
For schools migrating from another system or enrolling large batches at the start of a term, students can be imported in bulk via spreadsheet upload — saving hours of manual data entry.

## 2. Student Management

### Student Directory
A searchable, filterable directory of all enrolled students. Staff can quickly find any student by name, class, or status, and view their complete profile in one place.

### Student Profiles
Each student has a comprehensive digital profile including personal details, guardian contacts, enrollment history, documents, health records, and academic performance — all in one place.

### Promotion & Demotion
At the end of each academic year, administrators can promote students to the next class (or demote where necessary) in bulk or individually, with configurable promotion rules.

### Student Document Management
Upload, organize, and preview student documents — birth certificates, transfer letters, medical records, consent forms, and more. Documents are securely stored and accessible only to authorized staff.

### Student Health Records
Maintain health profiles for each student, including medical conditions, allergies, emergency contacts, and vaccination records — critical for school safety and compliance.

## 3. Academic Structure Management

### Classes & Sections (Arms)
Define your school's class structure — from nursery through senior classes — with multiple sections (arms) per class. For example, "JSS1 — A, B, C."

### Subjects Catalog
Create and manage all subjects offered by the school, including subject codes, descriptions, and assigned class levels.

### Teacher Allocation
Assign teachers to specific subjects and classes. The system tracks teacher workloads to ensure balanced distribution and avoid scheduling conflicts.

### Academic Calendar
Plan and publish the school year — term dates, holidays, examination periods, and special events. The calendar is visible to staff, students, and parents.

### Academic Structure Overview
A bird's-eye view of how your school is organized — classes, subjects, teacher assignments, and student counts — all on one screen for quick reference.

## 4. Continuous Assessment & Results

### CA (Continuous Assessment) Configuration
Define how continuous assessment works in your school — the number of CA tests, their weight relative to exams, and how they combine into final scores. Fully customizable to match your school's grading philosophy.

### CA Score Entry
Teachers enter CA scores for each student in their class. The system supports multiple CA tests per term and automatically calculates weighted totals.

### Result Computation
At the end of each term, the system automatically computes final results by combining CA scores with exam scores according to your configured weighting — no manual calculations or spreadsheet errors.

### Result Approval Workflow
Results go through an approval process before being published. Teachers submit, department heads review, and administrators approve — ensuring accuracy and accountability at every level.

### Broadsheets
Generate professional broadsheets showing all students' results across all subjects in a class — the traditional format schools have used for decades, now digital and printable.

### Transcripts
Generate individual student transcripts with a complete academic history. These can be printed or shared digitally with the student or other institutions.

### Result Publishing
Once approved, results are published directly to student and parent portals — eliminating the need to print and distribute report cards manually.

### Grading Policy & Grading Scales
Configure your school's grading system — whether it's A–F, percentage-based, or a custom scale. Different scales can be applied to different class levels if needed.

## 5. Examination Management

### Exam Timetabling
Create examination schedules that avoid clashes — no student scheduled for two exams at the same time. Exam timetables are published to student and parent portals automatically.

### Computer-Based Testing (CBT)
The platform includes a full CBT system that allows schools to conduct exams online — ideal for modern assessment practices and exam preparation for external CBT exams.

#### Question Bank
Build a reusable library of questions across all subjects. Questions support multiple formats including multiple choice, true/false, and short answer. Questions can be tagged by topic and difficulty for easy exam assembly.

#### Exam Creation
Assemble exams from the question bank in minutes. Set time limits, shuffle questions and options to prevent cheating, and assign exams to specific classes.

#### Live Exam Monitoring
During a CBT exam, administrators can monitor students in real time — see who has started, who is in progress, and who has submitted. Suspicious behavior (like switching browser tabs) is flagged automatically.

#### Exam Results & Analysis
View CBT results immediately after submission — no manual grading required. The system provides per-question analytics to identify topics where students struggled, helping teachers adjust instruction.

#### Exam Security
Configure security settings to maintain exam integrity — browser lockdown, question shuffling, time limits, and access restrictions. Prevent unauthorized access and cheating.

### Offline CBT Support
For schools with unreliable internet, CBT exams can be conducted offline and synced to the server once connectivity is restored — ensuring exams are never disrupted by network issues.

### Exam Item Analysis
Deep-dive into exam performance at the question level — identify which questions were too easy, too hard, or poorly written. This helps improve question quality over time.

### Certificate Verification
Issue digital certificates that can be verified online by third parties (employers, other schools) — adding credibility and preventing forgery.

## 6. Attendance Management

### Student Attendance
Teachers can mark daily attendance in seconds — by class, with one-tap present/absent for each student. The system tracks attendance rates over time and flags students with concerning absence patterns.

### QR Code Attendance
Students scan a QR code to mark themselves present — fast, contactless, and eliminates roll-call time. The system generates and displays QR codes for each class session.

### Batch Attendance Upload
For schools using external attendance systems (biometric devices, paper registers), attendance records can be uploaded in bulk via spreadsheet.

### Staff Attendance
Track staff attendance and punctuality separately from students. Staff can mark their own attendance, and administrators can review attendance patterns.

### Attendance Reports
Generate detailed attendance reports by student, class, staff member, or date range — useful for parent-teacher meetings and staff performance reviews.

### Absence Reason Tracking
Record and categorize reasons for absences (illness, family emergency, etc.) to build a complete attendance picture for each student.

### Attendance Audit Trail
Every attendance change is logged — who marked it, when, and any modifications — ensuring full accountability and preventing tampering.

## 8. Digital Learning & Virtual Classrooms

### Virtual Classrooms
Conduct live online classes within the platform — no need for separate video conferencing tools. Teachers can teach, students can participate, and sessions are integrated with the school's academic records.

### Classroom Discussions
Each virtual classroom includes a discussion forum where students can ask questions, share resources, and collaborate — extending learning beyond live sessions.

### Course Materials
Teachers can upload and organize course materials — lecture notes, slides, videos, reading assignments — organized by subject and class. Students access materials anytime from their portal.

### Private Lesson Requests
Students or parents can request private (one-on-one) tutoring sessions with teachers through the platform. Requests include preferred subject, schedule, and any special requirements.

### Private Lesson Approvals & Payments
Administrators review and approve private lesson requests. The system manages pricing, payment collection, and scheduling — creating a new revenue stream for the school with minimal administrative overhead.

### Virtual Learning Settings
Configure how virtual learning works — which classes offer online sessions, consent requirements, notification preferences, and integration settings.

### Parent Consent for Virtual Learning
Parents must provide consent before their child can participate in private lessons or certain virtual learning activities — ensuring parental awareness and compliance with child protection policies.

## 9. Finance & Fee Management

### Fee Structure Configuration
Define fee items (tuition, transport, boarding, uniforms, etc.) and assign them to specific class levels. Different classes can have different fee structures. Fee structures can be versioned, so historical records remain accurate even when fees change.

### Class-Level Fee Overrides
Apply special fee arrangements to individual students or groups — scholarships, discounts, sibling discounts — without changing the base fee structure.

### Payment Plans
Offer flexible payment plans to parents — installment options, due dates, and automatic reminders. This improves fee collection rates by making payment more manageable.

### Fee Collection & Payment Processing
Record fee payments manually or process them online through integrated payment gateways. The system supports multiple payment methods and automatically updates each student's fee balance.

### Bulk Payment Upload
For schools that collect payments through banks or external channels, payments can be uploaded in bulk and matched to student accounts automatically.

### Outstanding Fees Tracking
Real-time visibility into who owes what. The system tracks outstanding balances, sends automatic reminders to parents, and generates arrears reports for management.

### Invoice Management
Generate professional invoices for each student per term. Invoices can be printed, emailed, or viewed in the parent portal. Each invoice includes a detailed breakdown of fees charged.

### Receipt Generation
Automatically generate and issue receipts for every payment received. Receipts are professional, numbered sequentially, and available to parents through their portal.

### Payment Reversals & Adjustments
If a payment was recorded in error or a fee needs adjustment, administrators can reverse payments or apply adjustments with full audit logging — maintaining financial integrity.

### Fee Exemptions
Manage fee exemptions for scholarship students, staff children, or other special cases. Exempted fees are clearly tracked and excluded from outstanding balance calculations.

### Fee Ledger
A complete transaction history for each student — every charge, every payment, every adjustment — in one chronological ledger. This is the definitive financial record per student.

### Reconciliation
Match recorded payments against bank statements to ensure all funds received are properly accounted for. Discrepancies are flagged for investigation.

### Financial Reports
Generate a wide range of financial reports — revenue summaries, collection rates, outstanding balances, fee waivers, and more. Reports can be filtered by date range, class, or fee type.

### Finance Audit Log
Every financial transaction is logged with who, what, when, and why — providing a complete audit trail for accountability and external audits.

### Payment Gateway Integration
Connect to online payment providers so parents can pay fees directly from the parent portal using cards, bank transfers, or mobile money — no need to visit the school's bursary.

## 13. Security & Compliance

### Role-Based Access Control
Every user sees only what they're authorized to see. Administrators have full access; teachers see their classes; students see their own records; parents see their children's information. Access is controlled at a granular level.

### Session Management
Monitor and control active user sessions — see who is logged in, terminate suspicious sessions, and enforce automatic logout after inactivity periods.

### Data Encryption
Sensitive data — passwords, personal information, financial records — is encrypted both in transit and at rest, protecting against unauthorized access.

### Backup & Restore
The system supports regular data backups and point-in-time restoration — ensuring your school's data is never lost, even in the event of hardware failure or human error.

### Privacy Center
A dedicated privacy management area where data retention policies, consent records, and data subject requests are managed — supporting compliance with data protection regulations.

### Incident Management
Track and manage security incidents — unauthorized access attempts, data breaches, policy violations — with investigation workflows and resolution tracking.

## 22. Multi-School (Multi-Tenant) Architecture

For organizations managing multiple schools — such as school groups, franchises, or government education departments — the platform supports:

- **Independent school configurations** — each school has its own branding, fee structures, academic calendar, and user base
- **Centralized oversight** — a Super Admin portal provides visibility across all schools from a single dashboard
- **Shared infrastructure, isolated data** — all schools run on the same platform, but each school's data is completely isolated and secure
- **Scalable** — add new schools to the platform without purchasing additional software or infrastructure
