## Account & Login

### Q: How do I create a parent account?

A: Parent accounts are created by the school administration. Contact your school's admin office to request an account. You'll receive login credentials via email.

### Q: How do I reset my password?

A: 
1. Go to the login page
2. Click "Forgot Password?"
3. Enter your email address
4. Check your email for a reset link
5. Click the link and create a new password

### Q: What if I don't receive the password reset email?

A: 
1. Check your spam/junk folder
2. Wait a few minutes and try again
3. Make sure you entered the correct email
4. Contact support if the issue persists

### Q: Can I have multiple parent accounts?

A: No, each parent has one account. If you have multiple children, you can link them all to one account and switch between them.

### Q: How do I log out?

A: Click your name in the top right corner and select "Sign Out". Always log out when using a shared device.

### Q: Is my account secure?

A: Yes. We use industry-standard security measures including:
- Encrypted passwords
- Secure HTTPS connection
- JWT authentication tokens
- Regular security audits

## Children & Linking

### Q: How do I add another child to my account?

A:
1. Go to Profile
2. Click "Add Child"
3. Enter the child's ID or verification code
4. Click "Link Child"

### Q: How do I switch between my children?

A: Use the child selector dropdown in the header. Select the child you want to view.

### Q: Can I remove a child from my account?

A: Yes. Go to Profile → Linked Children → Click the remove button next to the child's name.

### Q: What if I can't find my child's verification code?

A: Contact your school's admin office. They can provide the verification code or link the child directly.

### Q: Can my spouse also access my child's information?

A: Yes, if they have a parent account. They can link the same child to their account using the verification code.

## Grades & Academic

### Q: How often are grades updated?

A: Grades are typically updated within 24 hours of being posted by the teacher. Some schools update more frequently.

### Q: What does GPA mean?

A: GPA stands for Grade Point Average. It's calculated on a 4.0 scale:
- A = 4.0
- B = 3.0
- C = 2.0
- D = 1.0
- F = 0.0

### Q: How is GPA calculated?

A: GPA is the average of all subject grades. For example, if your child has A, B, and A, the GPA would be (4.0 + 3.0 + 4.0) / 3 = 3.67.

### Q: Can I see my child's previous term grades?

A: Yes. Go to Academic Progress and select the term from the dropdown.

### Q: What are upcoming assessments?

A: These are tests, quizzes, and exams scheduled in the next 30 days. Check this section to help your child prepare.

### Q: How do I download an academic report?

A: Go to Academic Progress and click "Download Report". Select the format (PDF or Excel) and save to your device.

## Attendance

### Q: How is attendance calculated?

A: Attendance percentage = (Classes attended / Total classes) × 100

For example, if your child attended 18 out of 20 classes, attendance is 90%.

### Q: What's a good attendance percentage?

A: Most schools require 90%+ attendance. Check with your school for their specific policy.

### Q: What does "Excused Absence" mean?

A: An excused absence is an approved absence (e.g., medical appointment, family emergency). It may not count against attendance.

### Q: How do I report an absence?

A: Contact your school's admin office. They will mark the absence as excused if approved.

### Q: Can I see attendance trends?

A: Yes. Go to Attendance Tracking to see monthly attendance trends and patterns.

### Q: How do I download an attendance report?

A: Go to Attendance Tracking and click "Download Report". Select the format and date range.

## Messages

### Q: How do I send a message to a teacher?

A:
1. Go to Teacher Messages
2. Click "New Message"

A: Response times vary. Teachers typically respond within 24-48 hours during school days.

### Q: Can I delete a message?

A: No, messages cannot be deleted. They're kept for record-keeping purposes.

### Q: What if I don't get a response from a teacher?

A: Try sending a follow-up message or contact the school's admin office.

## Notifications

### Q: How do I control what notifications I receive?

A: Go to Notifications → Preferences. You can enable/disable notifications by type and channel (email, SMS, in-app).

### Q: Can I receive SMS notifications?

A: Yes, if SMS is enabled. Go to Notifications → Preferences and enable SMS for the notification types you want.

### Q: Why am I not receiving notifications?

A: Check:
1. Notifications are enabled in preferences
2. Your email and phone number are correct
3. Check spam/junk folder for emails
4. Contact support if the issue persists

### Q: Can I receive notifications for specific children?

A: Yes. Notifications are specific to the selected child. Switch children to see their notifications.

### Q: How do I mark all notifications as read?

A: Go to Notifications and click "Mark All as Read".
