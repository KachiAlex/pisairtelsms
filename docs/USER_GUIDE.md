## Welcome to ScholarX Parent Portal

The ScholarX Parent Portal gives you complete visibility into your child's academic progress, attendance, behavioral reports, communications, fees, timetable, health, and notifications—all in one place.

---

## Getting Started

### Logging In

1. Visit https://scholarx-app.vercel.app
2. Click "Parent Login"
3. Enter your email and password
4. Click "Sign In"

**Demo Credentials** (for testing):
- Email: `parent@demo.com`
- Password: `demo123`

### Your First Login

After logging in, you'll see the Parent Portal dashboard. Here's what you can do:

1. **View Dashboard**: See key metrics about your child
2. **Select Child**: If you have multiple children, use the child selector
3. **Navigate**: Use the sidebar menu to access different sections
4. **Manage Profile**: Update your account information

---

## Dashboard Overview

The dashboard is your home page. It shows:

- **Attendance**: Current attendance percentage
- **GPA**: Current grade point average
- **Outstanding Fees**: Amount due
- **Upcoming Exams**: Number of exams coming up
- **Recent Grades**: Latest grades posted
- **Recent Announcements**: Latest school announcements
- **Upcoming Events**: Important dates
- **Active Alerts**: Important notifications

### Understanding the Metrics

**Attendance**: Shows the percentage of classes attended. Aim for 90%+.

**GPA**: Grade Point Average on a 4.0 scale. Higher is better.

**Outstanding Fees**: Amount still owed to the school.

**Upcoming Exams**: Number of exams scheduled in the next 30 days.

---

## Switching Between Children

If you have multiple children in the school:

1. Click the **Child Selector** in the header
2. Select the child you want to view
3. The portal updates to show that child's information

All data displayed is specific to the selected child.

## Main Features

### 1. Academic Progress

View your child's grades and academic performance:

- **Grades by Subject**: See grades for each subject
- **GPA**: Current grade point average
- **Class Average**: How your child compares to the class
- **Performance Trends**: See improvement over time
- **Upcoming Assessments**: Know what's coming up
- **Download Reports**: Get detailed academic reports

**How to Access**: Click "Academic Progress" in the sidebar

### 2. Attendance Tracking

Monitor your child's attendance:

- **Attendance Percentage**: Overall attendance rate
- **Attendance Records**: Day-by-day attendance
- **Absence Reasons**: Why absences occurred
- **Attendance Trends**: See patterns over time
- **Download Reports**: Get attendance reports

**How to Access**: Click "Attendance Tracking" in the sidebar

### 3. Behavioral Reports

Track your child's conduct and behavior:

- **Conduct Grade**: Overall conduct grade
- **Incident Reports**: Any behavioral incidents
- **Positive Recognition**: Awards and achievements
- **Teacher Comments**: Feedback from teachers
- **Behavior Trends**: See patterns over time

**How to Access**: Click "Behavioral Reports" in the sidebar

### 4. Communications

Stay informed with school announcements:

- **Announcements**: Latest school announcements
- **Filter by Category**: Academic, events, etc.
- **Mark as Read**: Keep track of what you've seen
- **Download Attachments**: Get important documents
- **Search**: Find specific announcements

**How to Access**: Click "Communications" in the sidebar

### 5. Teacher Messages

Communicate directly with teachers:

- **Message Conversations**: View all conversations
- **Send Messages**: Contact teachers about your child
- **Message History**: See past conversations
- **Attach Files**: Share documents if needed
- **Delivery Confirmation**: Know when messages are read

**How to Access**: Click "Teacher Messages" in the sidebar

### 6. Fee Management

Manage school fees:

- **Fee Summary**: Total fees and amount paid
- **Fee Structure**: Breakdown of all fees
- **Payment History**: See all payments made
- **Payment Plans**: Set up installment plans
- **Download Receipts**: Get payment receipts
- **Online Payment**: Pay fees directly

**How to Access**: Click "Fee Management" in the sidebar

### 7. Timetable

View your child's schedule:

- **Weekly Schedule**: Class schedule for the week
- **Exam Schedule**: Upcoming exams
- **Holidays**: School holidays and breaks
- **Download**: Export to calendar (PDF/iCal)
- **Current Day**: See today's classes highlighted

**How to Access**: Click "Timetable" in the sidebar

### 8. Health & Wellness

Track health information:

- **Medical History**: Past medical records
- **Vaccination Status**: Vaccination records
- **Allergies**: Known allergies and reactions
- **Emergency Contacts**: Important medical contacts
- **Health Initiatives**: School health programs

**How to Access**: Click "Health & Wellness" in the sidebar

### 9. Notifications

Stay updated with notifications:

- **Notifications List**: All notifications
- **Filter by Type**: Academic, attendance, etc.
- **Mark as Read**: Track what you've seen
- **Notification Preferences**: Control what you receive
- **Email/SMS Settings**: Choose how to be notified

**How to Access**: Click "Notifications" in the sidebar

### 10. Profile Management

Manage your account:

- **Profile Information**: Your contact details
- **Edit Profile**: Update your information
- **Change Password**: Update your password
- **Linked Children**: Manage child accounts
- **Account Security**: Security settings

**How to Access**: Click "Profile" in the sidebar

## Common Tasks

### How to Pay Fees

1. Go to **Fee Management**
2. Click **"Pay Online"** button
3. Select payment method
4. Enter payment details
5. Confirm payment
6. Download receipt

### How to Contact a Teacher

1. Go to **Teacher Messages**
2. Click **"New Message"**
3. Select the teacher
4. Type your message
5. Click **"Send"**

### How to Download a Report

1. Go to the relevant section (Academic, Attendance, etc.)
2. Click **"Download Report"** button
3. Select format (PDF, Excel)
4. Save to your device

### How to Update Your Profile

1. Go to **Profile**
2. Click **"Edit Profile"**
3. Update your information
4. Click **"Save Changes"**

### How to Add Another Child

1. Go to **Profile**
2. Click **"Add Child"**
3. Enter child's ID or verification code
4. Click **"Link Child"**

### How to Change Your Password

1. Go to **Profile**
2. Click **"Change Password"**
3. Enter current password
4. Enter new password
5. Click **"Update Password"**

## Understanding Your Data

### Grades

- **A**: 90-100% (Excellent)
- **B**: 80-89% (Good)
- **C**: 70-79% (Satisfactory)
- **D**: 60-69% (Needs Improvement)
- **F**: Below 60% (Failing)

### Attendance

- **Present**: Attended class
- **Absent**: Did not attend
- **Late**: Arrived after class started
- **Excused**: Absence was approved

### Conduct Grades

- **A**: Excellent conduct
- **B**: Good conduct
- **C**: Satisfactory conduct
- **D**: Needs improvement
- **F**: Serious conduct issues

## Troubleshooting

### I Forgot My Password

1. Click **"Forgot Password?"** on the login page
2. Enter your email
3. Check your email for reset link
4. Click the link and create a new password

### I Can't See My Child's Data

1. Make sure you've selected the correct child
2. Check that the child is linked to your account
3. Try refreshing the page
4. Contact support if the issue persists

### The Portal is Loading Slowly

1. Check your internet connection
2. Try refreshing the page
3. Clear your browser cache
4. Try a different browser
5. Contact support if the issue persists

### I'm Not Receiving Notifications

1. Go to **Notifications** → **Preferences**
2. Check that notifications are enabled
3. Verify your email and phone number
4. Check your spam/junk folder
5. Contact support if the issue persists

## Security Tips

1. **Use Strong Password**: Use a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols
2. **Don't Share Password**: Never share your login credentials
3. **Log Out**: Always log out when done

5. **Update Password**: Change password regularly
