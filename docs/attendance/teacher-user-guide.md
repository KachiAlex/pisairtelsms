## Getting Started

### Accessing the Attendance Entry Interface

1. Log in to your teacher dashboard
2. Navigate to **Attendance** in the main menu
3. Click **Mark Attendance** to open the attendance entry interface

### Understanding the Interface

The attendance entry interface displays:
- **Date Selector**: Choose the date for which you're marking attendance (defaults to today)
- **Student List**: All students in your homeroom class
- **Status Buttons**: Quick selection for attendance status
- **Absence Reason**: Dropdown for recording why a student was absent
- **Bulk Actions**: Buttons to mark all students as present or absent at once

## Marking Attendance

### Step-by-Step Instructions

#### 1. Select the Date

- Click the **Date Picker** at the top of the form
- Choose the date for which you want to mark attendance
- **Note**: You can only mark attendance for today or past dates. Future dates are not allowed.

#### 2. Review Your Student List

The system displays all students in your homeroom class. Each student row shows:
- Student name
- Student ID
- Current attendance status (if previously recorded)

#### 3. Mark Individual Student Status

For each student, click one of these status buttons:

| Status | Meaning | Use When |
|--------|---------|----------|
| **Present** | Student was in class | Student attended the class |
| **Absent** | Student was not in class | Student did not attend |
| **Late** | Student arrived after class started | Student arrived late |

**Example**: To mark "John Doe" as present, click the **Present** button in John's row.

#### 4. Record Absence Reason (if applicable)

If you mark a student as **Absent**, you can optionally record the reason:

1. Click the **Absence Reason** dropdown in that student's row
2. Select from available reasons:
   - Sick
   - Family Emergency
   - Permission
   - Unauthorized
   - Other

**Note**: Recording absence reasons helps administrators identify patterns and provide targeted support.

#### 5. Review Your Entries

Before submitting, review all the attendance statuses you've entered. The form shows:
- Total students in class
- Number marked as present
- Number marked as absent
- Number marked as late

#### 6. Submit Attendance

1. Click the **Submit Attendance** button at the bottom of the form
2. A confirmation dialog appears showing:
   - Number of records to be saved
   - Summary of statuses (present/absent/late)
3. Review the summary and click **Confirm** to save
4. You'll see a success message: "X attendance records saved"

## Recording Absence Reasons

### Why Record Absence Reasons?

Recording absence reasons helps:
- Administrators identify patterns (e.g., frequent illness)
- Provide targeted interventions
- Distinguish between excused and unexcused absences
- Generate detailed absence reports

### Available Absence Reasons

| Reason | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Sick** | Student was ill |
| **Family Emergency** | Family emergency or urgent matter |
| **Permission** | Student had permission to be absent |
| **Unauthorized** | Student was absent without permission |
| **Other** | Other reason (specify if possible) |

### How to Record

1. Mark the student as **Absent**
2. Click the **Absence Reason** dropdown for that student
3. Select the appropriate reason
4. Submit attendance as normal

## Bulk Actions

### Mark All Present

To quickly mark all students as present:

1. Click the **Mark All Present** button at the top of the student list
2. All students will be marked as present
3. You can still modify individual students if needed
4. Submit attendance when ready

**Use Case**: When the entire class is present and you want to save time.

### Mark All Absent

To quickly mark all students as absent:

1. Click the **Mark All Absent** button at the top of the student list
2. All students will be marked as absent
3. You can still modify individual students if needed
4. Submit attendance when ready

**Use Case**: When the class is cancelled or all students are absent.

### Modifying After Bulk Action

After using a bulk action, you can still:
- Change individual student statuses
- Add absence reasons
- Correct any mistakes

Simply click the desired status button for any student to change their status.

## Viewing Attendance History

### Accessing Your Class Attendance

1. Go to **Attendance** in the main menu
2. Click **View History** or **Class Attendance**
3. You'll see a table of all attendance records for your class

### Understanding the History View

The attendance history shows:
- **Date**: When attendance was recorded
- **Student Name**: Name of the student
- **Status**: Present, Absent, or Late
- **Absence Reason**: Reason if applicable
- **Recorded By**: Who entered the attendance (you or another teacher)

### Filtering Attendance History

You can filter the history by:
- **Date Range**: Select start and end dates
- **Student**: Search for a specific student
- **Status**: Filter by present, absent, or late
- **Absence Reason**: Filter by specific reasons

### Viewing Class Statistics

Your dashboard shows:
- **Present Rate**: Percentage of students present
- **Absent Rate**: Percentage of students absent
- **Late Rate**: Percentage of students late
- **Total Records**: Total attendance records for the term

## Correcting Attendance Entries

### Modifying Past Attendance

If you need to correct a previous attendance entry:

1. Go to **Attendance** → **View History**
2. Find the record you want to modify
3. Click the **Edit** button for that record
4. Change the status or absence reason
5. Click **Save**
6. The system creates an audit trail entry showing what changed

**Note**: All changes are tracked in the audit trail for compliance and accountability.

### Viewing Change History

To see who changed an attendance record and when:

1. Click the **View Changes** link on any attendance record
2. You'll see a history of all modifications including:
   - Who made the change
   - When the change was made
   - What changed (old value → new value)

## Tips & Best Practices

### Best Practices

1. **Mark Attendance Daily**: Mark attendance at the same time each day to maintain consistency
2. **Record Reasons**: Always record absence reasons when available for better insights
3. **Review Before Submitting**: Double-check your entries before clicking submit
4. **Use Bulk Actions**: Use bulk actions when appropriate to save time
5. **Correct Mistakes Promptly**: If you make a mistake, correct it as soon as possible

### Tips for Efficiency

- **Keyboard Shortcuts**: Use Tab to move between students and arrow keys to select status
- **Bulk Actions First**: Use bulk actions as a starting point, then modify exceptions
- **Same Date Multiple Times**: You can submit attendance multiple times for the same date; the latest entry wins
- **Historical Entry**: You can mark attendance for past dates if you missed a day

### Common Mistakes to Avoid

- ❌ Marking attendance for future dates (not allowed)
- ❌ Forgetting to submit after marking (changes are lost if you navigate away)
- ❌ Not recording absence reasons (limits insights)
- ❌ Marking wrong status for a student (always review before submitting)

## Troubleshooting

### Issue: "Cannot mark attendance for future dates"

**Cause**: You're trying to mark attendance for a date in the future.

**Solution**: Select today's date or a past date using the date picker.

### Issue: "Student not found in class"

**Cause**: The student is not assigned to your homeroom or has been removed from the class.

**Solution**: Contact your administrator to verify the student's class assignment.

### Issue: "Attendance submission failed"

**Cause**: Network error or server issue.

**Solution**:
1. Check your internet connection
2. Try submitting again
3. If the problem persists, contact your administrator

### Issue: "Cannot edit attendance from more than 30 days ago"

**Cause**: The system restricts editing very old records for compliance.

**Solution**: Contact your administrator if you need to modify very old records.

### Issue: "Absence reason dropdown is empty"

**Cause**: No absence reasons have been configured by your administrator.

**Solution**: Contact your administrator to set up absence reasons.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Can I mark attendance for multiple days at once?

**A**: No, you mark attendance one day at a time. However, you can quickly mark attendance for multiple days by:
1. Marking attendance for day 1
2. Submitting
3. Changing the date
4. Repeating for day 2

### Q: What happens if I mark a student present and then change it to absent?

**A**: The system keeps the most recent entry. The previous entry is recorded in the audit trail for compliance.

### Q: Can I see which students are frequently absent?

**A**: Yes, go to your **Dashboard** and look for the **At-Risk Students** section. It shows students with attendance below 85% in the last 30 days.

### Q: Who can see my attendance entries?

**A**: Your attendance entries can be seen by:
- You (the teacher who entered them)
- School administrators
- Parents/guardians (only for their own children)

### Q: Can I undo a submission?

**A**: No, but you can edit the record after submission. Go to **View History**, find the record, click **Edit**, and make changes.

### Q: What if a student was present but I marked them absent by mistake?

**A**: Go to **View History**, find the record, click **Edit**, change the status to **Present**, and click **Save**.

### Q: Can I mark attendance for a student who is not in my class?

**A**: No, the system only shows students assigned to your homeroom. If a student should be in your class, contact your administrator.

### Q: How long are attendance records kept?

**A**: Attendance records are kept indefinitely for compliance and historical analysis.
